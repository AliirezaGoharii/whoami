01 — About

One architect.
A whole-product mindset.

I design and build end-to-end digital products — from the database schema to the last pixel. My core stack is Laravel on the server and Vue 3 on the client, joined by Inertia.js for a single-page experience without the usual SPA complexity.

Solo, I took ZenithCrypto — a financial super-app — from a blank page to a working platform, and shipped products like LinkHub. I treat performance, security and maintainability as features, not afterthoughts.

I work best when design and engineering are one conversation. Less handoff, fewer assumptions, and one person accountable for the whole outcome.

03 — The standard

High standards.
Practical decisions.

The principles behind the products — and the way I work with people.

01

Clarity before code.

Start with the problem, the constraints, and the outcome. Good technical decisions begin with understanding what matters.

02

One owner, end to end.

Design and engineering stay in the same conversation. One person connects the decisions and takes responsibility for the result.

03

The stack serves the problem.

Tools earn their place. I choose the architecture around the product’s needs, its constraints, and the people who will maintain it.

04

The details are the product.

Loading states, edge cases, validation, and the final interaction deserve the same attention as the main feature.

05

Progress you can see.

Working software, clear decisions, and honest conversations about scope. Make the trade-offs visible and keep the product moving.

06

AI drafts. I decide.

AI extends my capacity; judgment stays with me. I review the work, understand the choices, and own what ships.

From the first decision to the final detail: one connected process.
